# SIT Technology branding — theme 0.2.1

The close-up supplied by the owner makes the can's apple yellow, warm orange, crimson and silver the colour reference. The surrounding screenshot's purple/blue frame is not used. The implementation applies those colours to SIT's own identity and original architectural artwork.

| Role | Colour | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Apple yellow | `#f4d63b` | Dominant brand surface, utility bar, capability sections, editor token |
| Warm orange | `#f2a13b` | Hero artwork and broad yellow-to-orange section gradients |
| Light yellow | `#fff6c7` | Main page background and reading areas |
| Hero gold | `#ffda61` | Hero text backdrop and image transition |
| Crimson | `#b20d2f` | Primary actions and links, selected controls |
| Deep crimson | `#a51131` | Editorial and closing sections with white text |
| Charcoal | `#29282b` | Primary text on yellow and light surfaces |
| Silver | `#d7d6da` | Structural detail and chrome in the artwork |

Yellow/orange now dominate the homepage, capability section, page introductions and footer. Light surfaces around long copy and forms preserve readability. The typography remains self-hosted Manrope with an Arial fallback and the included SIL Open Font License.

## Original artwork

`wordpress/themes/sit-technology/assets/bridge-apple-gold.png` is an image-generation colour edit of SIT's existing bridge. The brief retained the geometry and camera, changed the glass to apple yellow and amber with orange/crimson reflections, retained silver trim and replaced the dark background with a luminous warm studio backdrop. It contains no beverage packaging, third-party logo or advertising copy.
