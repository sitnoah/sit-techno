# SIT Technology — consulting website research and redesign

Research and implementation: 6 September 2026. Release: theme 0.2.0 / Core 0.1.0.

## Design conclusion

SIT should help a prospective client answer three questions quickly: **Can you solve my problem? How would we work together? What should I do next?** The redesigned site brings those answers into the homepage and connects them to practical discovery tools.

The visual direction is an original SIT design: crimson and warm gold, a charcoal architectural hero, generous white space, self-hosted Manrope typography and restrained silver dividers. The headline **“Ambition, engineered.”** introduces UK leadership and African engineering without making unsupported claims about scale or results.

## What the reference sites do well

These observations come from public page content and navigation. They are not a browser screenshot audit, accessibility certification or analysis of the firms’ conversion performance. The adaptations are our design judgment; no proprietary artwork, page templates or client evidence has been copied.

| Reference | Observed pattern | Adapted for SIT |
|---|---|---|
| [Avanade](https://www.avanade.com/en-us) | Clear service and industry navigation; insights connect expertise with client stories and editorial material. Its [insights archive](https://www.avanade.com/en-nl/insights) provides industry, topic and content-type filters. | A grouped Expertise menu, searchable service catalogue and stronger links between services, industries and insights. SIT’s current small article collection does not need a complex filter system. |
| [Accenture](https://www.accenture.com/en/about/reinvention-services) | Broad capabilities are connected to business issues, industry context and delivery outcomes. | Three client ambition routes: modernise, create and scale. Each explains a useful first engagement and leads to a more focused enquiry. |
| [Andela](https://www.andela.com/) | Distinct routes for AI systems, engineers and workforce development; an AI assessment offers a useful step before a discovery call. | A five-question AI project planner and a clearer dedicated-team offer. The planner provides transparent qualitative guidance and does not claim to certify readiness. |
| [Topicus](https://topicus.nl/en/sectors) | Sector-led discovery links specific domain problems with solutions, cases and news. | An eight-sector explorer with problem statements, illustrative solution ideas and relevant service links. Target sectors are not presented as proven client experience. |
| [Thoughtworks](https://www.thoughtworks.com/) | Engineering knowledge, publications and insights are prominent parts of the proposition. | More visible editorial content, a technology toolkit and a practical delivery-assurance page. SIT needs a sustained publishing programme to develop equivalent depth. |
| [EPAM](https://www.epam.com/) | Services are grouped into recognisable disciplines; search, client work and editorial content support exploration. | Local site search, nine discoverable service offers, related service-detail links and a clear route to start a project. |

## Changes to the previous SIT design

The previous design established the company’s proposition and visual palette, but relied heavily on repeated cards. Services, industries and the delivery model were available mostly through linear reading. The redesign adds stronger hierarchy and more direct routes through that content.

- **Homepage:** a large charcoal hero, three quick routes, an interactive ambition explorer, concise expertise rows, a UK/Africa delivery section, an AI planner introduction and clearer editorial highlights.
- **Navigation:** a three-group Expertise disclosure, search dialog, company capabilities link, improved mobile menu and clearer enquiry action.
- **Service discovery:** keyword search and category filters work together across nine services, with counts, a useful empty state and reset controls.
- **Industry discovery:** keyboard-operable sector tabs, shareable fragment links and service connections across eight target sectors.
- **AI project planner:** five questions on purpose, data, ownership, controls and delivery. Incomplete fundamentals lead to focused discovery; mixed readiness leads to a pilot; all established fundamentals lead to controlled delivery planning. It uses no arbitrary numerical score, storage or signup.
- **Technology toolkit:** languages, frameworks and platforms grouped by the work they support. Relevant team capability must be confirmed for each engagement; vendor names imply no partnership.
- **Delivery assurance:** six practical topics for agreeing how a project will run. These are proposed working standards, not certification claims.
- **Company capabilities:** a concise overview with a working print / save-as-PDF action.
- **Service details:** related links make engagement models, assurance and toolkit information easier to find.
- **Typography and layout:** Manrope is served locally; larger text, calmer white sections and fewer repetitive cards give the content more room.
- **WordPress parity:** shared assets and allowlisted component shortcodes carry the interactive design into the installable theme. Public site search can include published, non-password-protected WordPress Pages and Posts.

## What still needs real business evidence

The strongest consulting websites support their offers with named specialists, verified client work and measurable outcomes. SIT should collect permissioned case studies, leadership bios, technical examples and an approved company information pack before adding these sections. The site deliberately contains no invented client logos, testimonials, project results, certifications or partner badges.

After a real WordPress staging deployment, the next design checks are browser/device review, keyboard and assistive-technology review, then observation of actual visitor journeys. Conversion uplift and accessibility conformance have not been measured or asserted.
